Privacy Policy

Content note

This policy is copied word-for-word from your old website and is dated 2017. We recommend reviewing and updating it before the new website goes live.

www.MitsuCap.com, owned and operated by MitsuCap Lease & Finance, Inc. (herein after “MitsuCap”) is committed to protecting your privacy. Because of this, we want to provide you with explicit information on how we collect, gather, and identify information during your visit to our site. This information may be expanded or updated as we change or develop our site. For this reason, we recommend that you review this policy from time-to-time to see if anything has changed. Your continued use of our site signifies your acceptance of our privacy policy.

Links to other websites.  Our web site contains links to other web sites that have no affiliation with MitsuCap and are operated by unrelated third parties. However, once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that MitsuCap does not have any control over other websites are not responsible for the content of or privacy practices of these web sites. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites and such sites are not governed by this privacy statement. You should exercise caution and look at the privacy statement applicable to the website(s) in question.

Personally Identifiable Information.  Refers to information that tells us specifically who you are, such as your name, company name, industry classification, phone number, email or postal address. In many cases, we need this information to provide the personalized or enhanced service that you have requested. The amount of Personally Identifiable Information that you choose to disclose to MitsuCap is completely up to you. The only way we know something about you personally is if you provide it to us in conjunction with one of our services.

What information do we collect and how do we use it?

MitsuCap collects information on our users in a variety of ways including, but not limited to:

  1. Your voluntary submissions, for example, if you sign up for our email newsletter
  2. Request for call back, request for information, request for quotation, credit application, or lease application
  3. Through cookie technology.

In an effort to provide personalized services, we may at times ask you to submit information such as your name, company name, industry classification, email address, phone numbers, addresses, financial and credit information, and references. You are never obligated to answer these questions, and you can decline this exchange of information at any time. However, if you should choose to withhold requested information, we may not be able to provide you with some of the services that may rely on the collection of this information. The information MitsuCap collects by way of this site is used in a variety of ways including, but not limited to, internal review; to improve the content of the site, to notify you about updates to the site; and to let you know about products and services that may be of interest based upon information you have provided.

We require this information to understand your needs and provide you with a better service, and in particular for the following reasons:

  1. Internal record keeping.
  2. We may use the information to improve our products and services.
  3. We may periodically send promotional email about new products, special offers or other information which we think you may find interesting based on your usage history of our site. This is accomplished by using the email address which you have provided.
  4. Surveys and/or offers
  5. From time to time, we may also use your information to contact you for market research purposes. We may contact you by email, phone, fax or mail.
  6. We may use the information to customize the website according to your interests.
  7. We may provide your information to our third-party partners for marketing or promotional purposes.
  8. We will never sell your information.

Email. If you have provided us your email address, MitsuCap periodically sends promotional emails to its subscribers about products and services. Your email address will not be sold, rented, shared, or distributed to any other company or organization with exception of the limitations defined herein.

Security. We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. In order to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure, we have put in place suitable physical, electronic and managerial procedures to safeguard and secure the information we collect online. www.MitsuCap.com is hosted on a secured server where public access is not allowed. Passwords, firewalls, and encryption technology protect the information about our customers which we collect and store.

With whom does MitsuCap share the information it gathers and tracks? We do not share your information with anyone with exception of our partners or affiliates in an effort to fulfill your specific requests. For example; lending institutions, assignees, transportation companies, leasing firms, 3rd. party maintenance service providers, product manufacturers, or similar.

Cookies. MitsuCap may automatically collect non-personally identifiable information and data through the use of “cookies.” Cookies are small files a web site uses to recognize repeat users, facilitate the user’s ongoing access to the site and facilitate the use of the site whilst providing us the information necessary to analyze web traffic. Cookies also allow a site to track usage behavior and compile site usage information that will allow for the improvement of content and target advertising. We only use this information for statistical analysis purposes and then the data is removed from the system. Overall, cookies help us provide you with a better website, by enabling us to monitor which pages you find useful and which you do not. A cookie in no way gives us access to your computer or any information about you, other than the data you choose to share with us. You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer. This may prevent you from taking full advantage of the website.

Your Consent. By using our Web site, you consent to the collection and use of the information described in this Privacy Policy by MitsuCap

Questions? We welcome your questions and/or comments. Please send all queries to info@mitsuCap.com
